A triangle with three right angles

On a sphere a triangle can have three 90° corners. Watch the walker trace it: down a meridian, a quarter-turn along the equator, then back up.

1 North Pole → Gulf of Guinea (down the Greenwich meridian)
2 Quarter-turn west along the equator → E. Pacific
3 Turn again, up the 90°W meridian → back to the Pole
angle sum = 270°  ·  spherical excess = 90°
